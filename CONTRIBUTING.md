# Contributing to Kizu

Thank you for your interest in Kizu.

Kizu is currently maintained solely by the author. External pull requests and
issues are not accepted and are closed automatically.

## Feedback, bug reports, and ideas

Please use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/kizu-lang/kizu/discussions)
for questions, bug reports, feedback, and design ideas.
