# Contributing to Kizu

Thank you for your interest in Kizu.

Kizu is currently maintained solely by the author, so external pull requests and
issues are not accepted and are closed automatically. Once Kizu enters the 1.0
maintenance phase and adoption grows, we will reconsider this policy.

## Feedback, bug reports, and ideas

Please share questions, bug reports, feedback, and design ideas in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/kizu-lang/kizu/discussions). The author
reviews posts there and decides what to address directly.
